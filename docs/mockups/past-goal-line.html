Today, time past an area's goal is drawn as extra squares. Two ways to make it a line instead: green when you went past an "at least" (good), red when you went past an "at most" (too much). The words stay, the "!" stays on red, so it never relies on colour alone.

A · A line after the squares, as long as the extra time

Learn · at least 4 h1h
✓ 3h30 above
Work · at most 4 h2h
! 2h over

B · A marker at the goal, then the line

Learn · at least 4 h1h
✓ 3h30 above
Work · at most 4 h2h
! 2h over

In both, the line's length is the extra time on the same scale as the squares, capped so it never pushes the words off the chip. The good line could be white (the text colour) instead of green if you prefer it quieter.
