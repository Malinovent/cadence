  • Tap an area to edit it. Only one is open at a time, and the rest of the list stays short.
  • − / ＋ buttons replace the number boxes, in half-hour steps, so no keyboard opens on the phone. "Between" shows two of them, from and to.
  • Colour swatches from the app's palette, plus one for any colour. They replace the small colour box.
  • The icon grid sits in the same panel, with no extra button to open it.
