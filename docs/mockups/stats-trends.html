Stats already shows the last 12 weeks per area. A trends feature could go further in three ways. Each is built only from what the app already keeps (every ticked block's area, time and date), and each describes, never judges: arrows and words, no red for having less.

A · Lines over time (my pick)

Each area's hours a week as a line, with its target dashed, over 12 weeks, 6 months or a year. Shows the shape: steady, rising, slipping.

No chart: the last 4 weeks against the 4 before, one calm line per area, the biggest changes first. Quickest to read.
