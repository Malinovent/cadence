Suggestions for Stats

  1. Bigger season squares with months under them, and tap one to see that week. Today they're tiny and dateless, so you can't tell which week a grace square was. my pick
  2. "Last week" becomes any week, with ‹ ›. Each area gets a bar with its target marked, and a calm "1 h short" tag in ink, like the area chips. Today a missed target is only a missing ✓, which is easy to overlook. my pick
  3. A "Last 12 weeks" trend per area: one small column per week with the target as a dashed line, and "kept 5 of 12". This is what a balance app should answer, "which area keeps slipping?", and nothing shows it today. my pick
  4. Time invested as hours a week, not totals. "Work 76 h" makes every other bar look tiny and can't be compared with a weekly target; "3.5 h a week" can. The ranges also become 4 weeks / 12 weeks / year, since "month" is really the last 30 days.
  5. Move "Reset stats" to Settings → Your data. It's destructive and rarely used, and a red button at the bottom of your stats is the wrong last thing to see.
  6. A real empty state: before anything is ticked, show what Stats will look like with a line saying "Tick blocks on the calendar and your weeks show up here", instead of rows of 0 h.

All of these use data the app already keeps: every ticked block is saved with its area, minutes and the moment you ticked it. Nothing new is stored. One catch for 2 and 3: a block counts in the week you tick it, so ticking last Friday's block on Monday puts it in this week. Worth fixing if per-week views become central.
