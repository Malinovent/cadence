In all three, the number comes first and the key word follows in bold: "4 h at least". The symbol also goes on the At least / At most / Between buttons when you open an area, so you learn it there.

The symbols are ink, never colour, and always sit beside the words, so they help you scan without replacing the text. If you like it, the same symbol could later go in the tooltip on each area chip under the calendar.
