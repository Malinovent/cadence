# Getting Cadence on your iPhone

Hi! Here's how to move your schedule over and get Cadence on your phone. It takes about five minutes.

## 1. Bring your current schedule (only if you've used Cadence before)

1. Open the attached file `cadence-export.html` in the **same browser** and the **same way** you usually open Cadence. For example, if you double-click `cadence.html` on your computer, put `cadence-export.html` in the same folder and double-click it.
2. Click **↓ Export my data**. A file named `cadence-backup-….json` is saved (usually in Downloads).
3. On your computer, open https://malinovent.github.io/cadence/
4. Go to **Settings** → **Import from file**, choose that file, then click **Yes, replace**.

## 2. Put Cadence on your iPhone

1. Open https://malinovent.github.io/cadence/ in **Safari** (it has to be Safari).
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
4. From now on, open Cadence from its new icon on your home screen. It opens full-screen and works even without internet.

## 3. Sign in so your phone and computer stay in sync

1. In Cadence, go to **Settings** → **Account & sync**.
2. Enter your email and tap **Send me a code**.
3. Open the email from Cadence (the first time, check your junk folder).
4. On your iPhone, **type the code** into Cadence. Don't tap the link in the email; it would open Safari instead of the app.
5. Tap **Sign in**.
6. Do the same on your computer (there you can tap the link or type the code).

Each device only needs this once; it stays signed in. If both already have a schedule, Cadence asks which one to keep. Pick the one with your real schedule.

## Tips

- **Hold** a category for a moment to drag it onto the week, or tap it and then tap a time.
- **Tap a block** to edit it, rename it or mark it done.
- Drag the small bars at the top or bottom of a block to make it longer or shorter.
- **Swipe sideways** to change day.
- Your changes sync a few seconds after you make them, and whenever you open the app.

Enjoy!
